# PinWall Help & Support

PinWall turns your own notes and images into an iPhone wallpaper. It supports portrait iPhone wallpapers on iOS 18 or later.

## Make your first wallpaper

1. Choose your original wallpaper image from Photos or Files. Avoid a Lock Screen screenshot, which already includes the clock and controls.
2. Add a note or image. Drag to move a sticker, pinch to resize, and use two fingers to rotate. Select a sticker to edit its text or style.
3. Use the layer controls to arrange overlapping notes and images.
4. Tap **Apply or Share** to save the finished PNG to Photos, share it, or set your wallpaper with Shortcuts.

Your current draft saves automatically on this device. Editing the draft does not automatically change the wallpaper already set in iOS; apply or export it again when you finish.

## Set your Lock Screen with Shortcuts

1. In PinWall, open **Settings → Set up Shortcuts** and tap **Add to Shortcuts**.
2. Choose **Shortcuts** in the system menu, then confirm **Add Shortcut**. If Shortcuts is missing from the menu, save the file to Files and tap the saved file to import it.
3. Return to PinWall and tap **Continue to wallpaper**. Choose a background and finish your layout.
4. Tap **Apply or Share → Set Wallpaper**. On the first run, allow the system prompts and choose a photo Lock Screen if asked.
5. Lock your iPhone to check the actual result.

The companion shortcut is named **PinWall Lock Screen**. If you rename it, enter its exact new name under **Set up Shortcuts → Need help?**. You can return to setup to reinstall a deleted shortcut.

If the wallpaper does not change, open the shortcut in Apple’s Shortcuts app. Check that **Set Wallpaper Photo** uses the image from **Get Prepared Wallpaper** and applies to your intended photo Lock Screen. Turn off **Show Preview** and **Crop to Subject** if these options appear, then try again from PinWall. Running this companion shortcut directly without a request from PinWall does not prepare a new wallpaper.

A successful return confirms that the shortcut completed; PinWall cannot read the system wallpaper to verify it. If there is no return or an error, check your Lock Screen, follow PinWall’s recovery message, and retry from the editor.

You can also save or share the PNG and set it manually in iPhone Settings. Exporting does not require Shortcuts or Pro.

## Photos, transparent images, and layout

- The wallpaper background and image stickers are separate. Replacing the background keeps your stickers.
- For a cutout sticker, import a transparent image you created in Photos or another app. PinWall styles the supplied image; it does not automatically remove its background.
- Clocks, widgets, notifications, and media controls may cover your content. Check the actual Lock Screen and move the stickers if needed.
- PinWall uses system pickers for the images you select. If permission to save to Photos is denied, use the share sheet or change the permission in iPhone Settings.

## PinWall Pro and purchases

The free plan includes one note and one image per wallpaper. The background does not count. All styles, saving, sharing, and Shortcuts are included.

Pro adds unlimited notes and images. **Lifetime** is a one-time non-consumable purchase. **Monthly** is an auto-renewable subscription with the same features. Current prices and renewal terms appear before purchase.

- To recover access, use the Apple Account that made the purchase and tap **Settings → Restore Purchases**. An internet connection may be needed.
- To manage or cancel the monthly subscription, tap **Settings → Manage Subscription**. Apple handles billing, renewals, and refunds.
- Buying Lifetime does not cancel an existing monthly subscription. Manage that subscription separately to avoid future renewals.
- If a subscription expires or a purchase is refunded, existing drafts remain editable and exportable. Adding new stickers follows the free limits.
- If a payment awaits approval, access unlocks after Apple approves and verifies it. A cancelled purchase does not unlock Pro.

## Local data and privacy

PinWall has no account or wallpaper server. Your draft stays on your device; deleting the app removes its local app data. Exported copies and the wallpaper already set in iOS remain separate.

**Settings → Clear local photo and draft** deletes PinWall’s saved draft, imported image copies, and generated files. It does not delete your original photos or exported wallpapers. Read the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) for more information.
