# PinWall Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 5 October 2026**  

PinWall lets you arrange your own text and images into an iPhone wallpaper. This policy explains how the app handles that information.

## 1. Information handled on your device

PinWall processes your wallpaper locally. It stores the following in the app's storage on your device so you can continue editing and create wallpapers:

- Text you enter, including text supplied to PinWall through Shortcuts.
- Images you choose from Photos, Files, drag and drop, or a PinWall shortcut action.
- Your wallpaper draft, including layout, styling, and screen dimensions used to render the image.
- App preferences, such as language, appearance, shortcut name, and shortcut setup and completion status.
- Generated wallpaper files and temporary information used to pass a wallpaper to Shortcuts or export it.

Imported image copies may retain metadata already contained in the supplied file. PinWall uses image information for decoding and rendering, not to profile you.

PinWall does not require an account or operate a server for storing your wallpapers. The app does not upload your text, images, or drafts to us. We do not sell this content or use it for advertising.

## 2. Photos and Files

PinWall uses Apple's system pickers to access images you select. It does not browse or read your entire photo library. Files can be supplied from a local or cloud storage location you choose in the system file picker.

If you choose to save a wallpaper to Photos, PinWall requests permission to add images. This permission is not used to read your photo library. You can manage permissions in iPhone Settings. Exporting through the share sheet remains available if you do not grant permission to add photos.

## 3. Shortcuts and sharing

When you choose to set a wallpaper, PinWall passes a request to Apple's Shortcuts app. The companion shortcut retrieves the generated image through a PinWall action and asks iOS to set your wallpaper. PinWall can also receive text and an optional image from shortcut actions you run.

The included companion shortcut is bundled with the app; PinWall does not upload your wallpaper to us to install or run it. Shortcuts you create or modify may use other apps or services according to the actions you add. Installed shortcuts can [sync through iCloud](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/shortcuts/-apdb3a4240b0/ios) when you enable Apple's sync setting.

Saving to Photos or choosing a destination in the share sheet sends the exported image to that destination at your direction. The exported image contains the text and images visible in your wallpaper. Other apps and services handle copies under their own privacy policies. Anyone who can see your Lock Screen may be able to read the wallpaper's contents.

## 4. Storage, retention, and deletion

Your current draft and its imported images remain in the app's local storage until you replace or delete them, clear the local draft, or delete the app. Temporary generated files and unused imported images are eligible for cleanup after they are more than one hour old; cleanup runs when the app performs its cleanup operation, rather than at an exact scheduled time.

Use **Settings → Clear local photo and draft** to delete PinWall's saved draft, imported image copies, and generated wallpaper request files. App preferences such as language and appearance are retained. This action does not delete your original photos, exported copies, installed shortcuts, or the wallpaper already set in iOS. Manage those separately in Photos, Files, Shortcuts, or iPhone Settings.

Deleting the app removes its local app data through iOS. Copies in backups, Photos, Files, or other destinations may remain. PinWall has no app-managed cloud sync, but [iCloud Backup may include app data](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/108770) depending on your Apple settings. We cannot access or delete your Apple backups.

## 5. Analytics, tracking, and Apple services

PinWall includes no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. The current version does not process in-app purchases or collect payment details.

Apple may process information when you use the App Store, Photos, iCloud, Shortcuts, or other Apple services. Depending on your device's analytics settings, Apple may also share app diagnostics or aggregated usage information with developers. These activities are governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and [App Analytics & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-analytics/).

Opening a web link, including the online privacy policy, makes a request to that website. Its operator and hosting provider may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and browser details.

## 6. Security and your choices

PinWall uses iOS app storage and system file protection for saved drafts and image files. You control which content you import, which wallpapers you share, and whether to enable relevant permissions and Apple cloud services. No storage or transfer method can guarantee absolute security.

If you voluntarily contact us, we receive the contact details and information you send, such as your email address, message, and attachments. We use that information to respond to your request. Do not send wallpaper content or other personal information that is unnecessary for your question. You can contact us to request deletion of information you have sent, subject to any applicable retention requirements.
